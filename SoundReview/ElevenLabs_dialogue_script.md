# Dialogue for ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io)

Make a free account, open **Text to Speech**, choose the model **Eleven v3** (it understands tags like [sighs] and [whispers]; if you only see Multilingual v2, leave the tags out).
Make each voice once with **Voice Design** (text description below), then use that voice for every line of that character.

Settings for all lines: **Stability about 30 to 40 percent** (lower = more human and varied), **Similarity about 75**, **Style about 40 to 60**, **Speaker boost off**.
Generate **4 to 5 takes of every line** and download each. WAV if offered, otherwise MP3.

## Old man (Voice Design description)
An elderly American man in his seventies. Gravelly, tired and dry, a little hoarse. Speaks quietly and close to the microphone. Bitter, resigned, worn out. No dramatic acting, just a man who has given up expecting help.

Lines (try each wording, 4 to 5 takes each):
1. `[sighs] Don't bother.`
2. `Don't... bother.`
3. `[exhales] Don't bother.`
4. `Oh... don't bother.`
5. thank you (when he takes the orange, a murmur): `[quietly] Thank you.` and `Mm... thank you.`

## Woman (Voice Design description)
A woman in her thirties. Soft and slightly shaky, out of breath, relieved after a scare. Speaks quietly and close to the microphone, a little breathy, sincere.

Lines:
1. `[exhales] Thank you.`
2. `Thank you... really.`
3. `[softly] Thank you.`
